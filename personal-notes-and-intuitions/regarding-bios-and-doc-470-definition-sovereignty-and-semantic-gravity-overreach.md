This also clarifies the difference between example and formalized communication.
Leading by example says:
Here is an action. See what you make of it.
Formalized communication says:
Here is the abstraction of the action. Here is how it should be understood, repeated, coordinated, judged, or transmitted.
That second move is powerful, but risky. The moment you formalize, you begin to lock the flow. You make the meaning more portable, but also more capturable. You gain coordination, but you risk dogma.

words, definitions, slogans, categories, doctrines, labels, arguments, policies, moral vocabularies, institutional phrases, public narratives.
It is not about private expression alone.

## Semantic Clamp Specification

Language is not only a Core 2 coordination tool.

It is also one of the ways Core 1 local ends become shareable.

A person speaks not only to define, govern, classify, or coordinate, but also to disclose what matters, what hurts, what is wanted, what is feared, what is loved, what is being carried, and what kind of life they are trying to preserve.

Communication therefore sits between Core 1 and Core 2.

It must make lived signal shareable enough to travel, but not so rigid that formalized language captures the signal and tells it what it is allowed to mean.

This creates a clamp-specification problem.

If the semantic clamp is too thin, communication becomes fog:

- vibes
- slogans
- undefined gravity
- manipulative confidence
- untraceable meaning
- words that pressure without declaring their load

If the semantic clamp is too thick, communication becomes domination:

- definition sovereignty
- category prison
- dogmatic vocabulary
- institutional overrule
- abstraction that locks the flow of meaning
- language that tells lived experience it does not count

A healthy semantic clamp is proportioned to the medium.

It gives enough structure for meaning to travel, coordinate, and be corrected, while preserving enough openness for listeners, cases, and lived gravity to update the meaning.

> The semantic trellis must carry meaning without owning meaning.
